Politiki y'Ibanga

Genda Neza · Yavuguruwe: 17/11/2026

Iyi politiki isobanura amakuru dukusanya muri app Genda Neza, impamvu tuyakusanya, uko tuyarinda n'uburenganzira ufite. App itegurwa na RUTEMBEZA Yves.

1. Amakuru dukusanya

  • Amakuru utanga: amazina, imeri, nimero ya telefoni (si ngombwa), akarere n'ubwoko bw'uruhushya witegura.
  • Amakuru y'imyigire: amasomo warangije, ibisubizo by'imyitozo n'ibizamini, intego ya buri munsi, n'ibyo wabitse.
  • Amakuru ya tekiniki: ubwoko bwa telefoni, verisiyo ya app na raporo z'amakosa, kugira ngo tunoze app.

App ni ubuntu ubu: ntidukusanya amakuru yo kwishyura.

2. Impamvu tuyakusanya

Kugufungurira konti no kuyirinda, kubika iterambere ryawe, kukwereka ibyo ugomba gusubiramo, kukoherereza imeri zo kwemeza konti no guhindura ijambobanga, no kunoza app.

3. Aho amakuru abikwa

Amakuru amwe abikwa kuri telefoni yawe, andi akabikwa kuri seriveri za Supabase, serivisi yo kubika amakuru ikoreshwa na app. Amakuru yoherezwa mu buryo bwizewe (HTTPS), kandi konti irindwa n'ijambobanga ryawe.

4. Abo dusangiza amakuru

Ntitugurisha amakuru yawe. Tuyasangiza gusa ababidufashamo mu kazi (nka Supabase ibika amakuru, na serivisi yohereza imeri), cyangwa inzego za Leta iyo amategeko abisaba.

5. Igihe tubika amakuru

Tubika amakuru yawe kugeza usibye konti yawe. Iyo usibye konti, amakuru yawe arasibwa.

6. Uburenganzira bwawe

Ufite uburenganzira bwo kumenya amakuru tugufiteho, kuyakosora, gusaba ko asibwa, no kwanga ko akoreshwa mu bikorwa bimwe na bimwe, nk'uko biteganywa n'Itegeko rirengera amakuru bwite n'ubuzima bwite mu Rwanda.

Ushobora gusiba konti yawe ubwawe muri app (Ibindi → Siba konti), cyangwa ukareba uko usiba konti n'amakuru yawe.

7. Umutekano

Dukoresha ingamba zikwiye zo kurinda amakuru yawe, harimo kuyohereza mu buryo bwizewe (HTTPS). Nta buryo na bumwe bufite umutekano ijana ku ijana, ariko dukora ibishoboka byose.

8. Abana

Abatarageza ku myaka y'ubukure bagomba gukoresha app babyemerewe n'ababyeyi cyangwa ababarera.

9. Impinduka kuri iyi politiki

Tuzakumenyesha muri app impinduka zikomeye kuri iyi politiki. Itariki iri hejuru ni yo ya nyuma yavuguruwe.

10. Twandikire

Ku bibazo bijyanye n'ibanga ry'amakuru yawe: yvesrutembeza@gmail.com

Summary (English)

Genda Neza is a free app for learning Rwandan road rules and preparing for the driving theory test, developed by RUTEMBEZA Yves.

  • Data we collect: name, email, optional phone number, district and licence category; learning progress (lessons completed, practice and exam answers, saved items, daily goal); basic technical information (device type, app version, error reports). We do not collect payment data.
  • Why: to create and protect your account, save your progress, send account emails (email confirmation and password reset), and improve the app.
  • Where it is stored: on your device and on Supabase servers, sent over HTTPS. We do not sell your data and share it only with service providers that help run the app (such as Supabase and the email delivery service) or authorities when the law requires it.
  • Retention and deletion: we keep your data until you delete your account. You can delete your account in the app (More → Delete account) or see how to request deletion.
  • Contact: yvesrutembeza@gmail.com
