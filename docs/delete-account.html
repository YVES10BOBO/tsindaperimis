Gusiba konti n'amakuru

Genda Neza · Yavuguruwe: 17/11/2026

Uburyo bwa 1: muri app (bwihuse)

  1. Fungura app Genda Neza hanyuma winjire muri konti yawe.
  2. Jya kuri Ibindi.
  3. Kanda Siba konti hanyuma wemeze.

Konti yawe n'amakuru ajyanye na yo birasibwa.

Uburyo bwa 2: ubinyuza ku imeri

Niba udashobora kwinjira muri app, twandikire kuri yvesrutembeza@gmail.com ukoresheje imeri wiyandikishijeho, maze wandike "Gusiba konti ya Genda Neza". Tuzasiba konti yawe tukubwire iyo birangiye.

Ibisibwa

  • Konti yawe (imeri, amazina, nimero ya telefoni, akarere, ubwoko bw'uruhushya).
  • Iterambere ryawe: amasomo warangije, ibisubizo by'imyitozo n'ibizamini, intego ya buri munsi n'ibyo wabitse.

Amakuru ya tekiniki atagaragaza uwo uri we (nka raporo z'amakosa) ashobora kubikwa igihe gito ngo tunoze app. Nta makuru yo kwishyura abikwa, kuko app ari ubuntu ubu.

How to delete your account (English)

In the app: sign in → More → Delete account → confirm. Your account and learning data are deleted.

By email: write to yvesrutembeza@gmail.com from the email you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Genda Neza account".

What is deleted: your account details (email, name, optional phone number, district, licence category) and your learning progress (lessons, practice and exam answers, daily goal, saved items). We store no payment data.
